Questions

USAT-COM FAQs

What subjects are in USAT-COM?

USAT-COM (Commerce) has a 30-MCQ subject section covering Commerce. Every category also has 20 Verbal Reasoning MCQs, 25 Quantitative Reasoning MCQs and an essay, according to published guides.

Who should choose USAT-COM?

USAT-COM suits business, commerce, accounting, finance and economics programmes. The subject section carries Accounting, Commerce and Economics. Start with the accounting equation and double entry, then move to trade, banking, insurance, demand and supply.

How should I prepare for USAT-COM?

Follow a 30-day plan: week one for syllabus and formulas, week two for subject MCQs, week three for quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning and essays, and week four for timed mock tests. Practise Commerce daily.
