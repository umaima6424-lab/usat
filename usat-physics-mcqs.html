Key Physics formulas

A short formula sheet for quick revision. See the full USAT formulas page.

Kinematics

  • v = u + at
  • s = ut + ½at²
  • v² = u² + 2as
  • Range R = u² sin 2θ / g

Force & Momentum

  • F = ma
  • p = mv
  • Impulse = FΔt = Δp
  • Friction f = μN

Work, Energy, Power

  • W = Fd cos θ
  • KE = ½mv²
  • PE = mgh
  • P = W/t = Fv

Circular & Rotational Motion

  • a꜀ = v²/r = ω²r
  • F꜀ = mv²/r
  • v = rω
  • τ = rF sin θ = Iα

Gravitation

  • F = Gm₁m₂/r²
  • g = GM/R²
  • Escape velocity vₑ = √(2gR)
  • Orbital speed v = √(GM/r)

Waves & Sound

  • v = fλ
  • T = 1/f
  • Beat frequency = |f₁ − f₂|
  • Doppler: f′ = f (v ± v₀)/(v ∓ vₛ)

Optics

  • 1/f = 1/p + 1/q
  • n = sin i / sin r
  • m = q/p
  • Fringe spacing Δy = λL/d

Heat & Thermodynamics

  • Q = mcΔT
  • PV = nRT
  • ΔU = Q − W
  • Efficiency η = 1 − T₂/T₁

Electricity

  • V = IR
  • P = VI = I²R
  • R series = R₁ + R₂
  • 1/R parallel = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
  • F = kq₁q₂/r²
  • C = Q/V

Magnetism & Induction

  • F = qvB sin θ
  • F = BIL sin θ
  • EMF = −NΔΦ/Δt
  • Vₛ/Vₚ = Nₛ/Nₚ

Modern Physics

  • E = hf
  • E = mc²
  • λ = h/p
  • N = N₀ (½)^(t/T½)
  • KEmax = hf − φ
Preparation tips

How to prepare USAT Physics

  • Learn formulas with units and practise numericals on motion, work and energy, circular motion and electricity.
  • Revise waves, optics and modern physics as concept questions. They are quick marks.
  • Check units and powers of ten before choosing an option.

USAT Physics FAQs

Which USAT categories include physics?

USAT-E, USAT-M, USAT-CS and USAT-GS include physics in the subject section.

How many physics MCQs should I practise?

Start with the 50 MCQs on this page, then repeat the topics where you lose marks. Aim for at least 70% before moving on to timed mock tests.

Are numerical questions asked in USAT physics?

Short numerical questions on motion, energy, circuits and waves are common, so practise them until you can solve them quickly.
