USAT Arts & Humanities FAQs

What subjects are in USAT-A?

USAT-A carries General Knowledge, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies in the subject section.

How do I prepare general knowledge for the USAT?

Read a reliable news summary every day, revise basic world and Pakistan facts, and take short quizzes weekly.

Which Pakistan Studies topics matter?

The freedom movement, important dates and resolutions, the constitution and the geography of Pakistan are the core topics.
