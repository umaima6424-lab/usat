USAT Essay Topics, Writing Tips and Quotations

The USAT essay part carries 25 marks and includes an argumentative task and a narrative task in published guides. Practise the topics below, follow the structure, and use the quotations to open or close your essays.

Structure

An essay structure that works

Argumentative

Introduction with a clear thesis. Three body paragraphs, each with one reason and one example. Address one opposing view. Finish with a firm conclusion.

Narrative

Start with a scene. Build to a turning point. Use the senses and short sentences. End with what you learned.

40-minute plan

5 minutes to plan, 30 minutes to write, 5 minutes to check spelling and clarity.

Questions

Essay FAQs

How do I write a good USAT essay?

Use a short introduction with a clear thesis, three body paragraphs with one example each, and a firm conclusion. The test has an argumentative task and a narrative task, so practise both. Published guides say you may write in English or Urdu, usually 400 to 500 words. Confirm the current instructions with HEC.

How long should a USAT essay be?

Published guides mention around 400 to 500 words for the descriptive part. Check the current HEC instructions.

What is the difference between argumentative and narrative essays?

An argumentative essay defends a clear position with reasons and examples. A narrative essay tells a personal story with a beginning, a turning point and a reflection.
