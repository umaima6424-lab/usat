USAT-GS FAQs

What subjects are in USAT-GS?

USAT-GS (General Science) has a 30-MCQ subject section covering Biology, Chemistry, Physics. Every category also has 20 Verbal Reasoning MCQs, 25 Quantitative Reasoning MCQs and an essay, according to published guides.

Who should choose USAT-GS?

USAT-GS is for general science programmes. The subject section is General Science, which draws on Biology, Chemistry and Physics. Balance your time across all three sciences and keep revising definitions and formulas.

How should I prepare for USAT-GS?

Follow a 30-day plan: week one for syllabus and formulas, week two for subject MCQs, week three for quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning and essays, and week four for timed mock tests. Practise Biology, Chemistry, Physics daily.
