Key Chemistry formulas

A short formula sheet for quick revision. See the full USAT formulas page.

Mole Concept

  • n = m/M
  • n = N/Nₐ
  • Molar volume at STP = 22.4 dm³
  • % yield = actual/theoretical × 100

Gas Laws

  • Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂
  • Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂
  • PV = nRT
  • Graham: r₁/r₂ = √(M₂/M₁)

Solutions

  • Molarity M = n / V(dm³)
  • Molality m = n / kg solvent
  • M₁V₁ = M₂V₂
  • ΔT_b = K_b·m

Acids, Bases & Equilibrium

  • pH = −log[H⁺]
  • pH + pOH = 14
  • Kw = [H⁺][OH⁻] = 10⁻¹⁴
  • Kc = [products]/[reactants]

Thermochemistry & Kinetics

  • ΔH = ΣH(products) − ΣH(reactants)
  • Rate = k[A]ᵐ[B]ⁿ
  • Half-life (1st order) = 0.693/k
  • ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

Electrochemistry

  • E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode
  • Q = It
  • Faraday: m = ZIt
  • Oxidation = loss of e⁻ (OIL RIG)

Organic Chemistry

  • Alkanes: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂
  • Alkenes: CₙH₂ₙ
  • Alkynes: CₙH₂ₙ₋₂
  • Alcohol –OH, Acid –COOH, Aldehyde –CHO
Preparation tips

How to prepare USAT Chemistry

  • Master the periodic trends, chemical bonding and the mole concept first.
  • Practise equilibrium, electrochemistry and acid-base problems with a formula sheet.
  • Revise organic functional groups and naming as short factual MCQs.

Questions

USAT Chemistry FAQs

Which USAT categories include chemistry?

USAT-E, USAT-M and USAT-GS include chemistry in the subject section.

Which chemistry topics matter most?

Chemical bonding, the periodic table, equilibrium, electrochemistry, hydrocarbons and functional groups are the safest topics to master first.

How should I revise chemistry for the USAT?

Make short notes of definitions and trends, memorise the main formulas, and solve MCQs daily so that facts stay fresh.
