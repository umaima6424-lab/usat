About the USAT

USAT 2026: what it is and how it works

A quick, clear introduction for students and parents.

The Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT) is a standardised admission test conducted under the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. Participating universities consider the USAT score when they admit students to undergraduate programmes. This means one test can support applications to several universities instead of preparing for a different entry test each time.

The test checks subject knowledge for your chosen category, plus Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning and essay writing. Because every category shares the reasoning sections and the essay, strong reasoning practice helps every student.

Topic-wise formulas

Keep these formulas ready for the USAT physics, chemistry, maths, quantitative reasoning and computer science sections.

Kinematics

  • v = u + at
  • s = ut + ½at²
  • v² = u² + 2as
  • Range R = u² sin 2θ / g

Force & Momentum

  • F = ma
  • p = mv
  • Impulse = FΔt = Δp
  • Friction f = μN

Work, Energy, Power

  • W = Fd cos θ
  • KE = ½mv²
  • PE = mgh
  • P = W/t = Fv

Circular & Rotational Motion

  • a꜀ = v²/r = ω²r
  • F꜀ = mv²/r
  • v = rω
  • τ = rF sin θ = Iα

Gravitation

  • F = Gm₁m₂/r²
  • g = GM/R²
  • Escape velocity vₑ = √(2gR)
  • Orbital speed v = √(GM/r)

Waves & Sound

  • v = fλ
  • T = 1/f
  • Beat frequency = |f₁ − f₂|
  • Doppler: f′ = f (v ± v₀)/(v ∓ vₛ)

Optics

  • 1/f = 1/p + 1/q
  • n = sin i / sin r
  • m = q/p
  • Fringe spacing Δy = λL/d

Heat & Thermodynamics

  • Q = mcΔT
  • PV = nRT
  • ΔU = Q − W
  • Efficiency η = 1 − T₂/T₁

Electricity

  • V = IR
  • P = VI = I²R
  • R series = R₁ + R₂
  • 1/R parallel = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂
  • F = kq₁q₂/r²
  • C = Q/V

Magnetism & Induction

  • F = qvB sin θ
  • F = BIL sin θ
  • EMF = −NΔΦ/Δt
  • Vₛ/Vₚ = Nₛ/Nₚ

Modern Physics

  • E = hf
  • E = mc²
  • λ = h/p
  • N = N₀ (½)^(t/T½)

How to prepare each USAT subject

How to prepare USAT Physics

  • Learn formulas with units and practise numericals on motion, work and energy, circular motion and electricity.
  • Revise waves, optics and modern physics as concept questions. They are quick marks.
  • Check units and powers of ten before choosing an option.

How to prepare USAT Chemistry

  • Master the periodic trends, chemical bonding and the mole concept first.
  • Practise equilibrium, electrochemistry and acid-base problems with a formula sheet.
  • Revise organic functional groups and naming as short factual MCQs.

How to prepare USAT Mathematics

  • Strengthen algebra, trigonometric identities and differentiation rules. These appear everywhere.
  • Practise integration, sequences and coordinate geometry with timed sets.
  • Solve each problem on paper. Do not rely on mental shortcuts for long steps.

How to prepare USAT Computer Science

  • Practise number system conversions and Boolean algebra until they are automatic.
  • Read short C++ or Python snippets and predict the output.
  • Revise operating system, network and database basics as definition MCQs.

How to prepare USAT Quantitative Reasoning

  • Cover all six areas: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, equations, statistics and scenario problems.
  • Learn percentages, ratios, averages, speed and work formulas by heart.
  • Skip any question that takes more than two minutes and return to it later.

How to prepare USAT Verbal Reasoning & Essay

  • Learn ten words daily with a synonym and an antonym, then revise them weekly.
  • Read one short passage every day and answer the questions without looking back.
  • Write one argumentative and one narrative essay each week within 40 minutes.

Your 30-day USAT preparation plan

A simple plan, practical test-day tips and answers to the questions students ask most.

Week 1

Foundations

Read the full syllabus, make a formula sheet and learn 10 new words every day.

Week 2

Subject drills

Solve 50 MCQs per subject. Note every mistake and fix the concept behind it.

Week 3

QR, verbal and essay

Daily quantitative practice, two comprehension passages and two timed essays.

Week 4

Mocks and revision

Take full 100-minute mock tests, then revise weak topics and formulas only.

Attempt every question

Published guides report no negative marking, so never leave an MCQ blank. Remove two options, then choose.

Manage your time

With 75 MCQs in about 100 minutes, aim for roughly 80 seconds per question. Skip long calculations and return later.

Write a structured essay

Use a short introduction, three clear body paragraphs with examples, and a firm conclusion.

Strengthen quantitative reasoning

Memorise squares, cubes and percentages. Practise mental maths and number series every day.

Your preparation checklist

USAT frequently asked questions

What is the USAT?

The Undergraduate Studies Admission Test (USAT) is a standardised admission test conducted under the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan. Participating universities consider the USAT score when they admit students to undergraduate programmes. Always read HEC's current notice for dates, registration and the list of universities.

Which USAT category should I choose?

Choose the category that matches your degree. USAT-E suits engineering, USAT-M suits medical and allied sciences, USAT-CS suits computer science and IT, USAT-GS suits general science programmes, USAT-COM suits business and commerce, and USAT-A suits arts and humanities.

What is the USAT test pattern?

Public guides describe 75 MCQs worth 75 marks, made of Verbal Reasoning (20), Quantitative Reasoning (25) and a Subject section (30), plus a 25-mark descriptive essay part, for 100 marks in total. Patterns can change, so confirm with HEC before test day.

Is there negative marking in the USAT?

Published guides state that there is no negative marking, so attempt every question. Check the latest HEC instructions to be sure.

What is the qualifying score?

Guides report a qualifying score of 50 out of 100. Universities may set higher merit requirements for competitive programmes, so check each university's admission policy.

What are the USAT subjects for each category?

USAT-E: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. USAT-M: Biology, Chemistry, Physics. USAT-CS: Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics. USAT-GS: Biology, Chemistry, Physics. USAT-COM: Accounting, Commerce, Economics. USAT-A: General Knowledge, Islamiyat, Pakistan Studies. Every category also has Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning and an essay.

How should I prepare for the USAT in 30 days?

Spend week one on the syllabus, formulas and vocabulary, week two on subject MCQs, week three on quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning and essays, and week four on timed full-length mock tests and revision. The preparation plan on this page has a checklist.

How can I improve my USAT quantitative reasoning score?

Practise all six areas (arithmetic, algebra and functions, geometry, equations, statistics and scenario-based maths), learn percentage, ratio, average, speed and work formulas, and solve the past-paper style QR set by topic with its worked solutions.

How do I write a good USAT essay?

Use a short introduction with a clear thesis, three body paragraphs with one example each, and a firm conclusion. The test has an argumentative task and a narrative task, so practise both. Published guides say you may write in English or Urdu, usually 400 to 500 words. Confirm the current instructions with HEC.

Where can I find USAT past papers?

HEC does not usually release full past papers. Use the syllabus-based practice here, including the past-paper style quantitative reasoning set, and look for any official sample material on HEC and Educational Testing Council (ETC) channels.

Can I take the USAT more than once?

Rules on repeat attempts, score validity and fees can change each year. Read the current HEC announcement and your target university's admission policy.

Is this website official?

No. USAT Prep Hub is an independent free study resource. It is not affiliated with HEC. Always use the official HEC and Educational Testing Council (ETC) channels for registration and results.
