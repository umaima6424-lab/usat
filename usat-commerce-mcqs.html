Questions

USAT Commerce FAQs

What subjects are in USAT-COM?

USAT-COM carries Accounting, Commerce and Economics in the subject section, along with Verbal Reasoning, Quantitative Reasoning and the essay.

Which programmes suit USAT-COM?

Business, commerce, accounting, finance and economics programmes typically suit this category. Check each university for its accepted category.

How do I prepare for accounting MCQs?

Practise the accounting equation, debit and credit rules, journal, ledger and trial balance until they feel automatic.
