USAT-CS FAQs

What subjects are in USAT-CS?

USAT-CS (Computer Science) has a 30-MCQ subject section covering Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics. Every category also has 20 Verbal Reasoning MCQs, 25 Quantitative Reasoning MCQs and an essay, according to published guides.

Who should choose USAT-CS?

USAT-CS is for students applying to computer science, software engineering, data science and IT programmes. The subject section carries Computer Science, Mathematics and Physics. Practise number systems, logic gates, algebra and calculus first because they reward rule-based practice.

How should I prepare for USAT-CS?

Follow a 30-day plan: week one for syllabus and formulas, week two for subject MCQs, week three for quantitative reasoning, verbal reasoning and essays, and week four for timed mock tests. Practise Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics daily.
